# Code of conduct

OpenIGI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every OpenIGI space: issues, discussions, pull requests, the mod index and the multiplayer servers the
project runs.

## In short

- Be welcoming and respectful, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Give and take criticism constructively.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or publishing someone's private information.
- Keep discussion about the project. Do not share or ask for copies of the original games; everyone brings their own.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately at the email address on
[@ShahriyarB's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ShahriyarB). Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues, mods and other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does, following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
